# Interview topics

Ask about each, one per message, with your recommended answer:

1. **Audience:** who uses the product, where, and on what.
2. **Personality:** 3 words the product should feel like, and 3 anti-words it must never feel like.
3. **References:** products or sites the user likes, and dislikes, with what to take or avoid from each.
4. **Brand assets:** existing logo, colors, fonts. Assets that exist are kept unless the user says otherwise.
5. **Profile:** showcase (landing page, portfolio) or app (dashboard, admin, CRUD). This picks the profile rules in the template.
6. **Density:** airy or compact.
7. **Light or dark:** one theme, or both.
8. **Motion appetite:** calm or expressive, and which motion libraries `AGENTS.md` allows.
9. **Signature moments:** the 2-3 places where the product should feel special. Everything else stays calm.
10. **Devices:** phone, desktop, tablet or others, and which one comes first.

Each answer becomes a line in `DESIGN.md`. When the user asks why you chose a value, state the reason in the file too.
